URUMI original demo illustrations

These nine SVG illustrations were drawn for this repository. They extend the original geometric mug, tote and chai artwork in the storefront audit. PNG files are local raster exports of the SVG sources. No stock imagery, remote fonts or external image services are used. Illustrations are demo artwork, not product photography.
